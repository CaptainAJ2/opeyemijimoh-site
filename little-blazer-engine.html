Little Blazer engine

  • MFET 16300
  • Siemens NX, Aras Innovator

Overview

I developed a complex assembly with Siemens NX.

For this project, I developed a fully parametric model of the Little Blazer internal combustion engine. The work covered the full lifecycle, from designing individual parts and building the assembly to storing and managing everything in a PDM system (Aras Innovator).

Little Blazer engine final top level assembly

Why it matters

Real products change many times, so the model has to change without breaking. Linking parts with expressions and tracking every change in a PDM system mirrors how companies manage product data.

Results

I moved beyond standard part modeling using Master Modeling techniques. I built over 25 inter-part expressions so that a change to one part would propagate automatically through the rest of the assembly.

To simulate a real product lifecycle, I managed the project inside Aras Innovator.

That meant tracking formal revision control and executing Engineering Change Orders (ECOs) to document the design history.
